# References

## Problem and exact methods
- H. Kellerer, U. Pferschy, D. Pisinger — *Knapsack Problems*, Springer, 2004. DOI 10.1007/978-3-540-24777-7.
- S. Martello, P. Toth — *Knapsack Problems: Algorithms and Computer Implementations*, Wiley, 1990 (Dantzig bound, branch and bound).
- G. B. Dantzig — *Discrete-variable extremum problems*, Operations Research 5 (1957) — LP relaxation bound.
- D. Pisinger — *Where are the hard knapsack problems?*, Computers & Operations Research 32 (2005) — instance families.
- J. Burkardt — `knapsack_01` datasets (P01).
